Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before we start your project.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us about your business, the type of website you need, the pages and features you have in mind, and your target deadline. We will discuss the scope and provide a quote.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive layouts are included in the packages so the website can adapt to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you build a website with a Python backend?

Python backend requirements can be discussed for custom projects. The exact features, hosting and database needs must be agreed before work begins.

How long will my website take?

Timing depends on the number of pages, features, content readiness and feedback. We will agree on a delivery schedule after reviewing your requirements.

Are domain names and hosting included?

They are not included in the listed starting prices unless specifically stated in your project quote. Any third-party charges will be explained before work begins.

Can you create an online store?

We can discuss a product catalogue and ordering experience. Shopping carts, payment processing and other advanced ecommerce features require a separate scope and quote.
